Vegetable Omelette Recipe

A 5 minute recipe that is healthy and perfect for breakfast




Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Spinach
  • White Onion
  • Bell Peppers
  • Cheese
  • Salt
  • Black Pepper

Instructions

  1. Whisk eggs and milk until it's smooth
  2. Melt half the butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat and add the chopped vegetables
  3. Cook until the vegetables are tender
  4. Pour in the remaining butter, switch to low/medium heat and pour the egg mixture into the skillet
  5. Tilt the pan to spread the eggs evenly
  6. Sprinkle with cheese and season with salt and pepper
  7. Fold and serve hot
