Info

Honkai: Star Rail is a free-to-play turn-based role-playing gacha game developed by miHoYo (published globally by HoYoverse). Released on April 26, 2023, for Windows, iOS, and Android, and PS5. It is the fourth main entry in the Honkai series.

Players take on the role of the Trailblazer, an amnesiac protagonist who joins the Astral Express, a mystical train traveling across the cosmos. Together with a diverse crew, the Trailblazer journeys through multiple worlds such as the frozen planet Jarilo-VI, the celestial ship Xianzhou Luofu, and the dreamlike Penacony to resolve disasters caused by mysterious objects called Stellarons and confront godlike beings known as Aeons.

The game features strategic turn-based combat, a deep narrative with anime-style cutscenes, and a gacha system for acquiring characters and equipment. It blends science fiction and fantasy, exploring philosophical themes through its Paths—ideological forces followed by individuals and civilizations.

Main Cast

The Trailblazer (You)

The Trailblazer is the playable main protagonist of Honkai: Star Rail. Awakened during the opening events of the game, they are found by March 7th and Dan Heng on Herta Space Station during the Antimatter Legion's invasion. The player gets to choose either Stelle (female) or Caelus (male), along with their Receptacle Codename.

When there is the chance to make a choice, make one that you know you won't regret...

March 7th

An enthusiastic girl who was saved from eternal ice by the Astral Express Crew, and has the unique ability of being able to use "Six-Phased Ice." When she awoke, she knew nothing of herself or her past, and decided to name herself after the date of her rebirth, "March 7th." She takes many photos using her camera in hopes of discovering a memento from her past.

Well would you listen to that! I saved everyone without causing any trouble! You're pretty awesome, March 7th!

Dan Heng

The cold and reserved train guard and archivist of the Astral Express. Wielding a spear named Cloud-Piercer, he joined the Express crew to escape his secluded past.

Even as we speak, farewells are happening throughout the universe. The grief that we share is real, but there's nothing special about it.

Himeko

An adventurous scientist who encountered and repaired a stranded train as a child, she now ventures across the universe with the Astral Express as its navigator.

Alright, crew! This world is the target of our next trailblazing expedition!

Welt

An animator by trade, Welt is a seasoned member of the Astral Express Crew and the former sovereign of Anti-Entropy who has saved Earth from annihilation time and time again. He inherited the name of the world, "Welt.".

The galaxy is vast beyond compare, containing an infinite number of possibilities. An individual's fate shouldn't be limited to a single path ordained by heaven

Reviews

Honkai: Star Rail received widespread critical acclaim upon its April 2023 launch, earning an 80/100 on Metacritic (PC) and landing in the 89th percentile on OpenCritic with a "Strong" rating.

IGN  9 / 10

Praised for its stellar storytelling and satisfying progression, the game was called a free-to-play RPG that could hook players for months without requiring them to spend a penny. IGN's Jess Reyes later named it the best free-to-play game of 2023.

A free-to-play RPG that might hook you for months — without making you feel like you have to pay.

PC Gamer  90 / 100

Reviewer Tyler Colp highlighted its impeccable visual style and strategic depth, praising the elastic tone that shifts from heroic space opera to dark comedy. Battles were described as moving at the speed of an action game despite being turn-based.

An impeccable turn-based RPG with a killer visual style — despite its gacha systems.

PlayStation Universe  9 / 10

Called a must-play 2023 release with a chance to become the next big thing in live service gaming. The quality of content at launch felt like a game already live for years, with turn-based tactics and customizable loadouts singled out as standouts.

Honkai: Star Rail is a must-play 2023 release — one of the better RPG releases in recent memory.

GamesRadar+  4 / 5

The colourful cast and mercurial tone were praised throughout. While reviewers acknowledged it doesn't reinvent the wheel, the highly polished presentation, accessible combat, and engaging writing more than set it firmly on the right track as a live service RPG.

A highly polished turn-based RPG — its colourful cast and mercurial tone set it on the right track.
